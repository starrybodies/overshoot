# Eurostat treatment destinations for OVERSHOOT

Acquired 19 September 2026. Two previously catalogued sources now have pinned
responses, normalization, browser artifacts, lineage and validation. No Site
checkout was edited during this research stream.

## Files to integrate

- `normalized/treatment.json`: national waste treatment, 584 country/year/scope
  groups, 35 countries plus two EU aggregates, 2004–2022. `TOTAL` and `TOT_X_MIN`
  are different views, not additive components. Latest available source update:
  23 September 2025. `TOT_X_MIN` treatment begins in 2010.
- `normalized/weee-treatment.json`: 184 country/year groups of source `EE6`
  totals, 31 national reporting geographies plus EU27, 2018–2023. Treatment
  itself is available for 30 countries; Bosnia and Herzegovina contributes only
  market/generation observations. Latest native update: 8 April 2026.
- The `*-latest.json` variants retain each country's own latest year with a
  reported treatment total. Latest never means every country has the same year.
- `sources.json`: full camelCase registry records replacing the existing
  `eurostat-waste-treatment` and `eurostat-weee` catalogue entries.
- `normalized/observations.parquet`: 20,502 canonical cells, including 52 absent
  confidential cells with native flags. The source WEEE equipment categories
  are retained here, even though browser charts use their native total.
- `normalized/observations.json.gz`: same canonical records in compressed JSON.
- `raw/`: exact source JSON-stat, metadata, Eurostat reuse policy and April 2026
  WEEE country notes. `snapshots.json` retains URLs, retrieval dates, SHA-256 and
  byte counts.

## Interpretation

The all-sector waste treatment table locates final treatment **inside** a
country. It includes imported waste and excludes exported waste. Its totals do
not balance against that country's waste generation because the boundaries,
timing and classification differ. It is not municipal waste alone and it does
not establish the fate of a particular traded scrap shipment.

Use the six exclusive treatment leaves `RCV_R`, `RCV_B`, `RCV_E`, `DSP_L`,
`DSP_I`, `DSP_OTH`. Earlier or confidential data sometimes provide only the
combined `RCV_R_B` or `DSP_L_OTH`. In that case use the combined source category
once, or show the missing split. Never add the total or an aggregate to its
children, and never recover confidential values by subtraction. EU totals are
rounded to 10,000 tonnes; native rounding differences remain unadjusted.

The WEEE destination operations are `TRT_NAT`, `TRT_EU_FOR`, `TRT_NEU`.
They locate reported treatment within a country, elsewhere in the EU, or
outside the EU. They provide no receiving-country or facility identity. They
also do not cover all uncollected e-waste. Recovery, recycling and reuse codes
overlap and cannot be stacked as mutually exclusive fates.

Country-specific warnings are encoded in `country_notes` and
`geography_exceptions`. **Germany's national-treatment cell includes confidential
exports; do not render it as an entirely domestic fate.** Hungary's location
refers to first treatment. These qualifications must accompany destination
charts. The pinned country notes provide additional methodology.

Examples for review: Ireland 2023 reports 63,946 t treated: 40,532 t nationally,
1,841 t in another EU state, 21,573 t outside the EU. Sweden's latest WEEE
record is 2021. Türkiye has suppressed treatment cells: absent values are not
zero. No percentage is generated by this pipeline.

## Reuse and acquisition

Eurostat authorizes reuse of its statistical data with attribution, including
commercial reuse, subject to its published exceptions. The normalized browser
artifacts exclude UK and Kosovo records because they are outside the current
EU/EFTA/official-candidate groups named in that policy. Their original source
cells remain in the restricted audit snapshot. Do not expose raw snapshots as
an indiscriminate commercial data download. EU aggregates keep their actual
codes and are never presented as global values.

All browser values retain a source ID, actual reference year, native operation,
scope, unit and stable source cell identifier. Original native unit `T` means
metric tonnes and therefore needs a factor of one. ISO3 mapping includes
Eurostat `EL → GRC`; unknown country codes fail the pipeline.

Reproduce offline:

```sh
python acquire.py
python validate.py
```

Dependencies are Python 3.10+, `duckdb` and `pycountry`. Use `--refresh` only to
intentionally replace source snapshots for a new release. The pipeline writes
the expanded canonical JSON temporarily before compressing it; committed
artifacts all remain below the repository's large-blob limit.
